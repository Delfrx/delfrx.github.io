Privacy Policy – Redelo SMS

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how the app Redelo SMS (the “app”) handles your data. It is published by Del Frx, reachable at delfrx99@gmail.com.

1. In short

  • Your SMS and contacts stay on your phone. The app does not send them to our servers.
  • Messages from conversations you move to the private space are stored in an encrypted database on your phone.
  • The app shows an advertising banner (Google AdMob) only on the Settings screens. No ads appear in your conversations or in the private space. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent first.
  • We do not sell your data.

2. Data the app accesses

The app is an SMS messenger. To work, it becomes your default SMS app and accesses:

  • Your SMS (reading, receiving, sending) and the reception of your MMS: show your conversations, send your messages, show delivery receipts, send to several recipients, reply from the notification, schedule messages and send the auto-replies you turned on.
  • Your contacts: show your correspondents’ names and choose who receives the auto-reply.
  • Your SIM card information (number and slot of the cards): let you choose which SIM card is used to send.
  • Your notifications: alert you when a message arrives. For private conversations, the notification is generic and shows neither name nor content.
  • Exact alarms: send a scheduled message at the chosen time.
  • Files you choose: during a backup, restore or conversation export, the app only reads or writes the file you select.
  • QR code scanning (camera, optional): scan a QR code. The analysis is done on your phone (ML Kit); no image is saved or sent, the app only keeps the text of the code.

3. Where your data is stored

  • Regular SMS stay in Android’s message database.
  • Messages from contacts you move to the private space are moved to an encrypted database belonging to the app. The encryption key is protected by the Android Keystore.
  • The secret code is never stored in plain text: only a fingerprint (hash) computed from the code is kept on the phone.
  • Your settings, message templates, favorites and blocked numbers are stored on the phone.
  • Android’s automatic system backup is disabled for the app.

4. Data sharing and third-party services

  • Google AdMob (advertising): the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show a banner on the Settings screens. Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, your interactions with ads, and diagnostic and crash data, to show ads, measure their performance, analyze app performance and prevent fraud. The content of your SMS and contacts is never sent to AdMob. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Google Play services: ad consent and QR code scanning components (on-device processing).
  • Theme catalog: the app may download a list of themes from our website delfrx.github.io (hosted by GitHub Pages). This request contains no personal data, but the host can see your IP address, like any web server.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising purposes, except as described above.

5. Advertising and consent

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent message is shown at first launch. You can change your choice later in the app settings. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Your data stays on your phone as long as you keep the app. Uninstalling it deletes the app’s database, including the private space and the secret code.
  • At any time you can delete messages, remove a contact from the private space, and delete your templates, favorites and blocked numbers from within the app.
  • Backup or export files you create are under your control; delete them yourself if you no longer want them.
  • Warning: if you forget your secret code, private messages cannot be recovered.

7. Security

Encryption of the private space, key protected by the Android Keystore, optional app lock with fingerprint, limited secret-code attempts, screenshot blocking and automatic exit from private mode. No method offers absolute security: also protect your phone with a screen lock.

8. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a country offering equivalent rights, you can request access to, correction or deletion of the data we hold, or object to its processing. Because your messages stay on your phone, we generally hold no data about you; write to us at delfrx99@gmail.com for any request.

9. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

10. Changes

We may change this policy. The update date above will then change, and we will inform users of any significant change.
